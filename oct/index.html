Marigold

That is the object I wanted to give you.

It represents the path the dead must take,
as that they are guided towards our realm for a mere second
It is with the time they are given,
they spend with the living

I wish I woulda given you that flower,
not for the sake of expectancy,
but the sake that in that moment I understood

The words I said on the phone were not my best,
and your voice did more than I could hear,
I admit I was foolish

I don’t want to cry,
and I think you tried to fight that,
one can only imagine,
until it happens,
where they lose a person close to them

I hope you still don’t eat dinner alone
